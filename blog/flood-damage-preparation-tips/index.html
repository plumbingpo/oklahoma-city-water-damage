How to Prepare for Flooding in Oklahoma City, OK

Home / Blog / Flood Prevention for Homeowners in Oklahoma City, OK

Nobody controls the weather, yet Oklahoma City, OK property owners can still get ahead of flood damage with a few proactive steps. Being prepared significantly softens the blow of a flood and may save you thousands on restoration. Here Oklahoma City Restoration shares the preparation steps that work best for homeowners in Oklahoma City.

Find Out How Flood-Prone You Are

Understanding your property's flood risk is the first step in preparation. FEMA publishes flood maps that show whether a designated flood zone includes your property. Keep in mind that the majority of flood claims come from areas not designated as flood zones, so a lower-risk location still deserves attention. Evaluate your property's topography, drainage patterns, and proximity to waterways that could overflow during heavy rain.

Protect Your Property

Key physical preparations include ensuring proper grading slopes away from your foundation, keeping gutters and downspouts clear and extending discharge at least 6 feet from the foundation, installing and maintaining a sump pump with battery backup, sealing foundation cracks and applying waterproof coatings, elevating electrical panels and HVAC equipment above potential flood levels, and storing important documents and valuables above ground level.

Getting Your Insurance in Order

A typical homeowner's insurance policy excludes damage from rising floodwater. Flood policies are sold by the NFIP and by private carriers, and they usually come with a 30-day wait before coverage begins, so look into one long before any forecast forces the issue. For insurance purposes, take video and photos of the property and what you own. Keep insurance documents accessible and know your coverage limits and deductible.

Your Plan for a Flood Emergency

Make your plan well ahead of any flood threat. Pack a go-bag and learn the routes you would take to evacuate. The contact information for your insurance agent should be within quick reach as well. Once flooding is predicted, document how the property looks beforehand, take valuables to an upper level, and switch utilities off if you receive instructions to.
