How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • A sump pump should be tried out before storms arrive, ideally with a backup battery installed.
  • Before anything goes wrong, store the phone number for an emergency restoration crew.
  • Learn the location of your breaker panel, which lets you safely kill power to a wet room.
  • Find your main water shutoff today and make sure everyone in the house can close it.
  • Put leak alarms beneath sinks and beside the dishwasher, washer and water heater.
  • In basements and other low areas, keep documents and valuables up on shelves.

When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Oklahoma City

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water has reached the electrical panel, appliances or power strips on the floor.
  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • You have no way to locate or get to the shutoff while water continues to pour.
  • Sewage has backed up, or the water is dark with a foul odor.
  • Carpet, drywall or hardwood has been soaked for more than a few hours.
  • You came home to water and do not know how long it ran.
